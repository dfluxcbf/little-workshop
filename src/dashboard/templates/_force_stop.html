Force stop settles this job as stopped without its pillar's agreement: use it when the job cannot be stopped and you know it is stuck (its worker died, its pillar restarted). What it means:

  • Its pillar is still asked to stop it, but whatever it was doing may carry on and is not undone: a branch, commits or files it changed stay as they are.
  • Its outcome is no longer recorded: if it finishes later, that is ignored.
  • The jobs waiting on it fail.
  • Its queue is released at once, so the next job of {{ job.queue_key or "its queue" }} may start while the old work still runs.
